# v7.1.12 operator guide

Keep the existing installation and state. No OAuth sign-in, enrollment, database rebuild or reinstall is required. Existing automatic-update consent governs delivery from v7.1.11.

Healthy running hosts wait for the usual safe empty-room controller transition. A running controller with only failed, positively closed parked slots can apply this repair after fresh repeated observations and ownership checks; its newly repaired workers then start normally. Explicit operator closure/maintenance and unknown ownership stay fenced. A stopped or paused host is not started by publication.

Ordinary freemod and tuned defaults are retained. Forced DT choices intentionally remain DT. Live community rooms gain the existing official notification socket and keep HTTP fallback. A cold personal-statistics lookup posts its answer when ready without another command. Shared results remain subject to current visibility and consent.

Use the retained protected entry for status, support, network-support, ratings-support and update status. An update being offered or downloaded is not proof of activation. Verify the installed version, both room links, fresh website observations and continued activity/rating/log acknowledgments after transition. Preserve state and reports if ownership or startup is uncertain; do not repeat room creation blindly.

The installer and optional repair launcher are available for a local supported repair if automatic delivery cannot act. Verify RELEASE_SHA256SUMS.txt and download the installer asset rather than GitHub's source ZIP. Fresh installs follow LIVE_AUTHORIZATION.md and NETWORK_OPERATOR_GUIDE.md using their own hosting account.
