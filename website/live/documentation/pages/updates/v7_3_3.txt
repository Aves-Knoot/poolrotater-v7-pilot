===== Taiko =====

The release adds ''taiko-fixed'' and ''taiko-auto''. Selection is normal-speed NoMod (NM) only. The prepared catalog contains 37,635 Ranked/Approved charts: 37,447 native Taiko charts and 188 conversions with official Taiko difficulty calculated from the original source file. Other statuses, unsupported modified variants and raw player research data are excluded.

Automatic difficulty uses Taiko profile PP and 16 aggregate calibration points derived from qualifying Taiko NM top-play exposure. The data cover 3,298 of 9,000 profiles; this is an exposure heuristic, not a skill, playability or enjoyment guarantee. Missing profile/calibration data leaves the configured range in effect.

Taiko ratings and personal statistics use a disjoint ruleset identity. Rating upload can succeed while shared-snapshot refresh is delayed; a displayed total comes only from a verified snapshot containing the vote. Retry and reconciliation work independently from gameplay and command handling.
